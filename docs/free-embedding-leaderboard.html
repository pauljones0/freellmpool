Free embedding leaderboard

Which free embedding route retrieves best? freellmpool measures its own free embedders head-to-head on a fixed retrieval fixture — paraphrased queries against keyword-trap distractors — scored through the gateway itself. Winner as of 2026-09-19: Cloudflare bge-small, the only perfect score.

Ranks by recall@3, then MRR; exact ties break by measured latency. Mistral and OVH tied on accuracy (1.000 / 0.950) — Mistral ranks second on speed. An earlier OVH run hit its daily allowance cap mid-benchmark and was re-measured clean; failed routes score 0 rather than silently dropping out.

Method

24 fixed documents (12 topical passages + 12 keyword-trap distractors sharing vocabulary with the wrong topic) and 10 paraphrased queries, each with a known relevant document. Every route embeds all documents and queries through the pool; documents rank by cosine similarity; recall@3 and MRR average over queries. Fixture: src/freellmpool/rag_bench_fixture.json (v1, committed, fixed).

RAG uses the winner by default

freellmpool rag index embeds with the leaderboard winner unless --embed-model overrides it:

$ freellmpool rag index ./notes --store /tmp/demo.sqlite3
Indexed 1 chunks from 1 file(s) (embeddings: @cf/baai/bge-small-en-v1.5)
